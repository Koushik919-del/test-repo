THE SOKOVIA ACCORDS

Official UN Document 4-U93J

In accordance with the United Nations' concern regarding the unregulated actions of "enhanced individuals," the following protocols are hereby established as international law:


1. Any individual exhibiting extraterrestrial, enhanced, or superhuman abilities shall henceforth be considered a living weapon of mass destruction.

2. Said individuals may not operate within the borders of any sovereign nation without explicit authorization from that nation's governing body and the United Nations Panel.

3. The use of abilities for personal vendettas, unauthorized peacekeeping, or property alteration is strictly prohibited.

4. Failure to comply with these accords will result in immediate detainment at the Raft maximum-security facility.


These accords are not negotiable. The age of unchecked vigilantism is over.
